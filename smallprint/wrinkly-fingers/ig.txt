Why do fingers wrinkle in water?

Small Print #5. It isn't water soaking in: your nerves narrow the blood vessels in your fingertips (Kareklas et al., Biology Letters, 2013). Why? One test found wet marbles moved in 12% less time with wrinkled fingers (2013); another found no difference (Haseleu et al., PLoS ONE, 2014).
