Do goldfish really have a 3-second memory?

Small Print #7. No. In a lab, goldfish learned to press a lever for food that came one hour a day, and kept pressing at that hour for days after the food stopped (Gee et al., Journal of the Experimental Analysis of Behavior, 1994). Their memories run to weeks, even months, says fish researcher Culum Brown (Live Science, 2021).
