Why do apples turn brown?

Small Print #8. Cutting breaks the apple's cells, and an enzyme in them meets oxygen from the air and makes brown pigments (UW–Madison, 2008). Lemon juice slows it: its vitamin C does the work (Illinois Extension, 2020).
