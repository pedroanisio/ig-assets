Does tapping a shaken can stop it from spraying?

Small Print #9. In a Danish trial of about 1,000 cans of beer, shaken cans lost about the same whether they were tapped or not (Sopina et al., 2019, a preprint). Shaking makes bubbles, and they need minutes to dissolve again (Live Science, 2026). So set it down and wait.
