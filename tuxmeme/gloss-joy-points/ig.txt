What if every tiny thing you did was being scored?

Gus holds a door: plus two. Gus breathes, heavily: minus. Tamsin has nine million Joy Points and is saving up for more points, because points can be redeemed for more points. The balance does not roll over. Neither do you.
A Gloss sketch, a satire of cheerful forever-worlds hosted by a deadpan orb, voiced with ElevenLabs premade voices Alice (Sunny), Bill (Gus) and Laura (Tamsin); the Plaza and every character are original, drawn in code.

Voices: ElevenLabs premade voices Alice, Bill and Laura. Music composed with ElevenLabs Music. Original characters, drawn in code.
