Ever been asked to rate a place you can never leave?

The eternal stay survey: one to five. Tamsin gives five forever. Gus gives zero, which is not an option, so it is recorded as one. Comments go straight into the bin. Based on your feedback, the Plaza has added more plaza, which was the complaint.
A Gloss sketch, a satire of cheerful forever-worlds hosted by a deadpan orb, voiced with ElevenLabs premade voices Alice (Sunny), Sarah (Nadia), Bill (Gus) and Laura (Tamsin); the Plaza and every character are original, drawn in code.
