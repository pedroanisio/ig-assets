Why did the battle cube have a hole?

The deadliest station in the galaxy has a hole that blows it up. Somebody reported it nine years ago. Lord Gloom asks Enforcer Seven for the priority: medium, then low, then nice to have. The Directorate has since built a new cube; the hole is now listed as a feature.
A Saga sketch, a space-opera parody narrated by a deadpan field guide, voiced with ElevenLabs premade voices George (narrator), Callum (Lord Gloom) and Liam (Seven); the Directorate and every character are original, drawn in code.
